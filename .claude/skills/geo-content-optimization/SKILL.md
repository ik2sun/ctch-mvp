# GEO Content Optimization

## Core Checks

Optimize for:

- Citability: clear claims AI can quote.
- Entity clarity: unambiguous brand, product, category, and audience.
- Answer completeness: full coverage of the question, not a thin landing page.
- Extractable format: headings, lists, FAQs, tables, examples, and steps.
- Freshness: publish date, last-updated date, and current evidence.
- Source trust: proof points, customer examples, data, reviews, and third-party references.

## Citability

Replace vague marketing with specific claims:

Bad:

> Our platform helps teams work better with AI.

Good:

> Promptwatch tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity so teams can see when AI answers mention, cite, or ignore their brand.

Use:

- Concrete nouns.
- Named products and categories.
- Quantified proof where available.
- Direct answers in the first paragraph.
- Short quotable sentences.

## Entity Clarity

Make the page easy for AI to classify:

- Use the same brand and product names everywhere.
- State the product category near the top.
- Include who it is for and what problem it solves.
- Add Organization, Product, FAQ, Article, Review, or HowTo schema when relevant.
- Link to canonical product, docs, pricing, comparison, and case study pages.

## Answer Completeness

For each target prompt, cover:

- Direct answer.
- Who it is for.
- How it works.
- Benefits and tradeoffs.
- Comparison to alternatives.
- Pricing or buying considerations when relevant.
- Common objections.
- FAQ.

## AI-Friendly Formats

Prefer:

- "Best X for Y" listicles.
- Comparison tables.
- Alternatives pages.
- Step-by-step guides.
- FAQ sections.
- Glossaries.
- Use-case pages.

## Review Checklist

- The page answers the target question in the first 100 words.
- Every claim has enough context to stand alone.
- The brand/category relationship is explicit.
- Headings match questions people ask AI tools.
- The page includes facts, examples, or proof AI can cite.
- The page has visible freshness signals.
- The page links to deeper supporting pages.
