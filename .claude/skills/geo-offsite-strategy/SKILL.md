# GEO Offsite Strategy

## Why Offsite Matters

AI engines often trust third-party sources more than a brand's own site. Review sites, community threads, industry publications, videos, and comparison pages can influence which brands get recommended and cited.

## Source Types

Prioritize:

- Review sites: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, App Store, marketplace reviews.
- Comparison/listicle pages: "best X for Y", "top X tools", "X alternatives".
- Industry publications: expert quotes, contributed data, guest articles.
- Communities: Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, niche forums.
- YouTube and podcasts: tutorials, reviews, and category explainers.
- Partner pages: integrations, marketplaces, ecosystem listings.

## Reddit And Communities

Do:

- Find subreddits and threads where buyers already ask category questions.
- Answer with direct experience, tradeoffs, and concrete detail.
- Disclose affiliation.
- Add value even when the answer is not your product.

Do not:

- Astroturf.
- Drop links without context.
- Post fake testimonials.
- Over-optimize anchor text.

## Review Sites

Improve:

- Category fit and profile completeness.
- Recent review volume.
- Specific use-case language in reviews.
- Competitive comparison pages where buyers evaluate alternatives.

## Outreach Framework

1. Find pages AI engines already cite for your category.
2. Check whether the brand is missing, outdated, or mispositioned.
3. Pitch a specific correction or inclusion.
4. Provide proof: customer count, feature data, comparison facts, screenshots, or market evidence.
5. Track whether the page later appears in Promptwatch citations.

## Content Angles That Earn Mentions

- Original data.
- Benchmark reports.
- Clear category definitions.
- Templates and calculators.
- Practical how-to guides.
- Fair comparisons with tradeoffs.

## Output For Strategy Work

Produce:

- Priority source list.
- Reason each source matters.
- Recommended angle.
- Proof needed.
- Outreach risk or compliance note.
- Measurement plan using Promptwatch citation and visibility data.
